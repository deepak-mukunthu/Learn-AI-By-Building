# Your first 30 days: day-by-day checklist

- [ ] **Days 1 to 2.** Choose the problem. Measure how it's handled today (volume, minutes per request, errors). *Done when:* you have three baseline numbers.
- [ ] **Days 3 to 4.** Fill in the job card. Show it to one person who will use the assistant. *Done when:* they say "yes, that's the job."
- [ ] **Days 5 to 7.** Gather and clean five documents. *Done when:* each has a title and a date.
- [ ] **Days 8 to 10.** Write the instructions and build the first version. *Done when:* it answers the three easiest questions.
- [ ] **Days 11 to 13.** Write ten test questions and run them. *Done when:* you have a first score.
- [ ] **Days 14 to 18.** Fix one failure a day, rerunning all ten each time. *Done when:* the score is steady at 8 of 10 or better.
- [ ] **Days 19 to 21.** Add five more tests, mostly "not covered" and trick cases. *Done when:* you have 15 tests.
- [ ] **Days 22 to 25.** Pilot with three to five users. Read every conversation. *Done when:* you have logged each failure.
- [ ] **Days 26 to 28.** Fix the worst failures and add them as tests. *Done when:* no failure of the "must never" kind.
- [ ] **Days 29 to 30.** Compare to your baseline. Write one page: what worked, what didn't, what next. *Done when:* you can show numbers to your manager.

## Before you upload anything

- [ ] I'm allowed to share this document with this tool.
- [ ] It contains no personal details (names, salaries, health information), or I removed them.
- [ ] I know who can use the assistant and whether they can see the source files.
- [ ] I know whether the platform uses uploaded files to train its models.
- [ ] I know how to take the assistant down, and who will know it has been.
- [ ] If pricing is flat-rate, I know the monthly limits.
