# Cost and benefit worksheet

Fill in your own numbers. The example column is invented and only shows the arithmetic.

| Line | How to get it | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Requests per month | From your logs | 10,000 |
| B. Share the assistant resolves alone | From the pilot, not a guess | 40% |
| C. Minutes a person spends per request today | Measure ten | 6 |
| D. Cost per person-hour, fully loaded | Ask finance | $40 |
| E. Savings per month | A x B x C / 60 x D | 10,000 x 0.4 x 6 / 60 x $40 = $16,000 |
| F. Model and tool cost per month | Cost per conversation x A | $0.04 x 10,000 = $400 |
| G. Build and upkeep, per month | People's time, spread over a year | $6,000 |
| H. Net benefit per month | E - F - G | $9,600 |

Then stress the number: cut B in half and double F and G. If the result is still positive, the case is solid. If not, measure your least certain assumption first.
